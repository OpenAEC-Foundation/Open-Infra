# NLCS tables

The files in this directory are derived from the publication of the
**Nederlandse CAD Standaard (NLCS) 5.02** by DigiGO / Stichting NLCS:
<https://github.com/nl-digigo/NLCS> (`tabellen/publicatie`), licensed under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Changes: converted to tab-separated files and reduced to the columns Open
Infra uses (objects with their per-status colours, lineweights and linetypes;
colour palette; linetypes; statuses; disciplines; main groups; the NLCS-BGT
5.0 mapping). Regenerate with `tools/nlcs_tables.py`.
