## Versie 8 (de laatste, 64 s)

- Opening: "Open Infra in Open CAD Studio — One drawing: 3D city model, terrain & design"; daarna
  "…and technical drawings — 2D to NLCS: base map, cables, sheet & legend".
- **2D DRAWINGS**: Drawing sheet: legend & title block · BGT & cadastre on NLCS layers · KLIC cables & pipes · Contour
  lines from elevation data · Length profiles · Cross sections & earthworks
- **WORLDWIDE**: Any place on earth, any coordinate system · 600+ map services: Paris cadastre · Cologne: aerial photo
  & parcels · Zurich: WFS parcels with attributes · CityGML & CityJSON city models
- **THE NETHERLANDS**: Zoom out: aerial photo of the region · Natura 2000 · Geomorphological map · Topotijdreis: every
  map since 1815 · HisGIS: the cadastre of 1832
- **3D & CIVIL**: 3D city model from open data · KLIC cables & pipes in 3D · See-through ground: CPT soil columns ·
  Cables & pipes, seen from below · Alignments, corridors & daylighting · AI design assistant (MCP)
- "…and much more": Shapefile · GeoPackage · GeoJSON · KML/KMZ · GPX · GML · CSV · GeoTIFF · ASC grids · LAS/LAZ ·
  COPC point clouds · CityGML · CityJSON · LandXML · DWG/DXF · IMGeo-GML · KLIC/IMKL · BRO CPTs & boreholes · WMS ·
  WFS · XYZ & ArcGIS tiles · OGC API · OpenStreetMap · EPSG.io · NLCS check · Quantities · BGT mutation · Drawing
  sheets · Vehicle library · Swept paths · Water level · Terrain tiles · Python & MCP API · NL/EN
- Eindkaart: "Free & open source · worldwide", github.com/OpenAEC-Foundation/Open-Infra
